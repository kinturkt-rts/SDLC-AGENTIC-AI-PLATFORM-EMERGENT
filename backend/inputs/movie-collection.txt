We're looking to build a web application that helps users manage their personal movie collection. Users should be able to keep track of movies they've watched, maintain a watchlist, rate movies, and search their collection.

The application should provide an easy way to organize movies and quickly find information about them.

Features
Add and manage movies
Create a watchlist
Mark movies as watched
Rate and review movies
Search by title, genre, or year
Filter watched and unwatched movies
View recently added movies
Dashboard with basic movie statistics
Success

Users can maintain their movie collection, track what they've watched, and easily search or organize their movies.
