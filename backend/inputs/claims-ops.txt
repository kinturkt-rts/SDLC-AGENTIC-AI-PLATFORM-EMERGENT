We’re looking to build a web application that helps a regional insurance carrier manage policyholders, policies, claims, adjusters, payments, and compliance reviews from one central platform.
The application should be built with FastAPI and should expose a well-structured API that can later support web, mobile, and internal operations tools.

Scenario
The carrier currently uses separate systems for policy administration, claims intake, adjuster assignment, and claim payments. This creates duplicate records, inconsistent validation, and limited visibility into the full claim lifecycle.
The new system should provide a unified claims operations layer while preserving complete history for financial and audit purposes.

Features
Maintain policyholder profiles with personal or business details, contact information, identity references, risk tier, and status
Support individual and commercial policyholders
Create and manage insurance policies (auto, home, commercial) linked to policyholders
Track policy status, coverage limits, deductibles, effective dates, and premium amounts
Intake claims against active policies with incident details, loss type, reported date, and initial reserve
Assign claims to adjusters and track investigation status
Support claim states such as Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Partially Paid, Paid, Denied, Reopened, and Closed
Record claim payments, reserves, recoveries, and fee adjustments without deleting original financial entries
Allow authorized reviewers to approve, deny, or escalate high-value claims
Record review notes, approval decisions, and denial reasons
Handle claim reopenings and supplemental payments while preserving the original claim history
Maintain coverage limits and remaining available indemnity per policy
Create service tasks for document requests, inspections, salvage, and subrogation follow-ups
Assign tasks to adjusters or operations teams
Track task status, priority, owner, comments, and resolution details
Search and filter policyholders, policies, claims, payments, adjusters, and tasks
Provide operational summaries for open claims, aging claims, pending reviews, and high-value payouts
Maintain complete audit history for important actions and state changes
Seed realistic sample policyholders, policies, claims, payments, adjusters, and tasks

Important Business Rules
Policy numbers and verified email addresses must be unique where applicable.
Cancelled or lapsed policies cannot accept new claims except when explicitly reopened for a prior covered loss.
Claim amounts must be positive and cannot exceed remaining coverage after deductible unless an authorized override is recorded.
A claim payment cannot be issued until the claim is Approved or Partially Paid.
High-value claims above a configurable threshold require additional review before payment.
Completed financial claim payments cannot be edited or deleted; corrections use reversal or adjustment entries.
Duplicate claim submissions with the same idempotency reference must not create duplicate claims or payments.
Denied and closed claims must remain available in history.
Service tasks must follow valid status transitions.
Sensitive policyholder and claim data must only be available to authorized roles.
Every change to policy status, claim status, reserves, payments, and reviews must be auditable.

Must have for v1 — staff web UI
A working React + TypeScript browser UI (Vite) for staff use — this is required, not optional
Build the frontend with React, TypeScript, and Vite
Use Tailwind CSS for all styling (utility classes)
Use shadcn/ui components for forms, tables, buttons, dialogs, and layout
Do NOT use hand-written CSS files, CSS modules, styled-components, or inline style objects for layout/theming
Staff must be able to sign in, manage policyholders/policies/claims, and review payments from the UI

Success
The system is successful when staff can onboard policyholders, issue and manage policies, intake and investigate claims, approve or deny payouts, process payments safely, manage follow-up tasks, and retrieve operational summaries without losing financial or audit history — all from the Tailwind-styled React UI.
