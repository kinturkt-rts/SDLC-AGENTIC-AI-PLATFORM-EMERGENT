Hi Team,

We're currently maintaining visitor entries in a paper register at reception, which has become difficult to manage. We'd like a simple internal application that allows our reception team to register visitors before they arrive, check them in when they reach the office, and check them out when they leave.

Employees should also be able to pre-register expected visitors so reception already has their details. We'd like to capture visitor information, host employee, purpose of visit, company name, arrival/departure time, and badge number.

Features
Employee can pre-register visitors.
Reception can check visitors in and out.
Dashboard showing visitors currently inside the building.
Search visitor history.
Visitor badge assignment and return.
Email notification to the host when the visitor arrives.
Visitor status (Expected, Checked In, Checked Out, Cancelled).
Daily visitor report.
Basic admin page to manage employees and badge numbers.
