Library Catalog — books, members, and loans with date-aware business logic

Client story:
A small corporate library lets employees borrow physical books from a shared shelf. Librarians
manage the catalog; members check out and return books. The system tracks loan periods, overdue
status, and a simple hold queue when every copy is out — no external integrations, just a
working backend and Swagger for demos.

Features:
- Book catalog: ISBN, title, author, number of physical copies on shelf.
- Member registry: name, email; each member gets a personal access key at signup (shown once).
- Checkout and return with due date based on a configurable loan period (default two weeks).
- Availability: a title is borrowable when active loans are fewer than total copies.
- Per-member loan cap (e.g. five active loans max).
- Hold queue when unavailable: FIFO; when a copy returns, the longest-waiting hold is notified
  in the sense of being eligible to check out next (fulfilled hold record).
- Members cannot hold a title they already have checked out.
- Overdue is derived from due date vs today — not stored as a permanent flag column.
- Librarians manage books and members; members manage their own loans and holds.
- Anonymous users can browse/search the catalog only.

Personas (who needs what):
- Librarian — add/edit/remove books and members; full catalog access.
- Member — checkout, return, my loans, place holds.
- Anonymous — search/browse catalog, health check.

Business rules worth getting right:
- Due date computed at checkout from loan-period setting.
- Return triggers hold fulfillment for that book when applicable.
- Cannot delete a book with active loans.
- Member sees recent history plus all active loans on "my loans" view.
- Search supports partial match on title and author; optional filter to available titles only.

Demo data expectations:
- Multiple books, several members, active loans and holds to exercise FIFO and overdue logic.

Out of scope:
- Late fees, email notifications, barcodes, external library systems
- Per-member roles beyond member vs librarian
