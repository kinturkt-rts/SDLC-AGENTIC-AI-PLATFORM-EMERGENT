Hi Team,

We'd like an internal application where employees can recognize coworkers for outstanding work, teamwork, innovation, or customer support. HR should be able to review recognitions, approve reward points, and generate monthly recognition reports.

The application should have a Streamlit frontend and a FastAPI backend.

Features
Employee recognition submissions
Recognition categories
Reward points
Manager approval
Monthly leaderboard
Employee recognition history
Dashboard showing top performers
Search and filtering
Monthly recognition reports
Badge management
Department-wise analytics
CSV export
Role-based access (Employee, Manager, HR)
Audit history
Important business rules
Employees cannot recognize themselves.
Recognition must include a category and justification.
Reward points can only be approved by managers or HR.
Rejected recognitions remain in history.
Monthly leaderboards should include only approved recognitions.
HR can modify reward point values.
Technical expectations
FastAPI backend
Streamlit frontend
Persisted database
CRUD APIs
Multiple related entities
Seed demo data
Health endpoint
Success

Employees recognize colleagues, managers approve recognitions, HR manages rewards, and Streamlit dashboards display leaderboards, analytics, and recognition trends.
