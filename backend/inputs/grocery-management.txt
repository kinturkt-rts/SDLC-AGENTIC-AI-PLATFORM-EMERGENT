Build a small internal Grocery Product Inventory API for maintaining products and available stock.

Problem:

Product quantities and prices are currently updated manually in spreadsheets.
Store staff needs one place to manage products and track available inventory.
Internal tools should use a clean API — no browser app required for v1.

Who uses it:

Internal users can browse active products.
Inventory staff with a valid API key can create, update, restock, or deactivate products.
Health checks stay open for ops.

MVP features:

Create a product: product code, name, category, quantity, unit price, supplier, and active status.
View one product or list all products.
Update product details, quantity, and price.
Deactivate a product instead of permanently deleting it.
Filter products by category, supplier, active status, or low stock.
Seed a few sample grocery products.

Business rules:

Product code must be unique.
Product name, category, and product code are required.
Quantity and unit price cannot be negative.
Inactive products cannot receive stock updates.
Deactivated products remain available in history.
Writes without a valid API key should be rejected.

Must have for v1:

Persisted data.
Basic CRUD using FastAPI.
Swagger/OpenAPI is sufficient.
No Streamlit, SSO, or customer-facing UI.

Nice to have later:

Expiration dates, purchase orders, suppliers, stock alerts, sales tracking.

Constraints:

Internal use only.
Keep secrets out of source code.

Success:

Staff can add a product, list inventory, update its quantity and price, filter low-stock products, deactivate it, and still retrieve its history.
