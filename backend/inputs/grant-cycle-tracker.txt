Grant Cycle Tracker — nonprofit tracks opportunities, applications, awards, and reporting deadlines

Client story:
Priya is the grants lead at a mid-size nonprofit. Opportunities live in email, drafts in
shared drives, and reporting deadlines on sticky notes. They miss renewals and scramble when
a funder asks for a progress report. She wants one place to see every grant from “we might
apply” through “we must report,” with clear owners and due dates — not a full CRM.

What the product should do:
- Track grant opportunities: funder name, program title, focus area, estimated award size,
  and application deadline.
- Move each opportunity through a simple lifecycle: researching → drafting → submitted →
  awarded / declined / withdrawn.
- For awarded grants: record award amount, start/end dates, and required reporting milestones
  (what is due, when, and who owns it).
- Attach plain notes and checklist items to an opportunity or award (no fancy document
  management — just “what still needs doing”).
- Surface a “coming due” view: applications due soon and reports overdue or due this month.
- Let leadership see a portfolio snapshot: how many in flight, dollars awarded this year,
  and at-risk deadlines — without editing grant records.

Who uses it:
- Grants coordinator — create and update opportunities, submit status, manage reporting tasks.
- Program lead — own specific applications/reports; update notes and mark tasks done.
- Executive director — read-only portfolio and deadline risk view.

Rules that matter in real life:
- An opportunity can’t jump to “awarded” without going through submitted (or a clear exception
  note for rare cases staff explicitly allow).
- Reporting milestones only exist once a grant is awarded.
- Closing or withdrawing an opportunity should keep history for learning (“why we walked away”).
- Overdue reports should stay loudly visible until marked complete.
- Dollar amounts are for planning conversations — keep them simple and editable by coordinators.

Demo data expectations:
- Mix of opportunities across stages, including a few awards with reporting milestones.
- At least one overdue report and one application due within a week.
- A couple of funders with more than one opportunity over time.

Out of scope:
- Writing the grant narrative with AI, funder portals login, budgeting modules, donor CRM
