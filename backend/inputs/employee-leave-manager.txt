Employee Leave Manager for HR and people managers.

Client story:
James in HR says about 120 employees request PTO by email; managers reply “approved” with no
central record. Payroll needs balances and a “who is out today” view. This should replace email
for the pilot.

Problem:
- Leave requests and approvals live in email with no reliable history.
- Nobody trusts PTO/sick balances or knows who is out today by team.
- Managers must not approve their own leave — those need an HR path.

Who uses it:
- Employees submit and cancel their own pending leave; see their balance and history.
- Managers approve or deny direct reports and see a team calendar — not their own requests.
- HR admins see everything, adjust balances with a reason, and approve when the requester is a manager.
- Sign-in with username and password and roles (employee, manager, HR admin). Health checks stay open.

MVP features:
- Submit leave (PTO, sick, or unpaid) for a date range; support half-day or full-day per date.
- Approval workflow: pending → approved or denied; employees can cancel while still pending.
- Approved PTO/sick deducts from the employee’s balance; unpaid does not.
- Team calendar and “who is out today” for managers and HR.
- HR balance adjustments with a note and an audit trail.
- Streamlit (or similar) UI: submit leave, my requests, team calendar (role-gated), HR adjustments.
- Seed several departments, a manager chain, mixed balances, and requests in various statuses for demos.

Business rules:
- Cannot request more PTO/sick hours than available balance at submit time.
- Block overlapping approved leave for the same person.
- Half-day and full-day hours are fixed and consistent (e.g. 4 vs 8 for the demo).
- Only the employee’s manager or HR can approve; nobody approves their own request.
- Denying or cancelling before approval does not change balances; approving writes a clear ledger entry.

Must have for v1:
- Persisted data, login with roles, and an audit trail for balance changes.
- A usable internal UI for the pilot — not email-only.

Nice to have (later):
- Workday sync, Outlook calendar, accrual by tenure, email notifications.

Constraints:
- Internal pilot only.
- Keep secrets out of source code.

Success:
- An employee submits PTO; their manager approves and the balance drops; HR sees who is out today; a manager’s own request goes to HR instead of self-approval.
