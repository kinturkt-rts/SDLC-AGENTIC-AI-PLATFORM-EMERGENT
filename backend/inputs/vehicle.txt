Hi Team,

We need an internal application to manage the company's fleet of vehicles used by employees for client visits, deliveries, and office travel. Right now, vehicle bookings, maintenance schedules, and service history are maintained across multiple spreadsheets, making it difficult to know vehicle availability and upcoming maintenance.

The application should provide a Streamlit interface backed by a FastAPI API. Fleet administrators should manage vehicles and maintenance, while employees should be able to reserve available vehicles.

Features
Register company vehicles
Vehicle categories (Car, SUV, Van, Truck)
Vehicle booking and reservation
Driver assignment
Service and maintenance scheduling
Fuel log management
Insurance and registration expiry tracking
Vehicle availability dashboard
Maintenance history
Search and filtering by vehicle, driver, status, and location
CSV report export
Role-based access (Admin, Fleet Manager, Employee)
Audit history
Important business rules
A vehicle cannot have overlapping bookings.
Vehicles under maintenance cannot be reserved.
Maintenance history cannot be deleted.
Insurance expiry must always be after the registration date.
Only Fleet Managers can approve reservations and schedule maintenance.
Technical expectations
FastAPI backend
Streamlit frontend
Persisted database
Multiple related entities
CRUD operations
Seed demo data
Health endpoint
Success

Fleet managers can register vehicles, employees can reserve available vehicles, maintenance schedules prevent future bookings, and dashboards provide fleet utilisation and maintenance status
