Build a healthcare clinic assistant — a FAQ chatbot with Streamlit UI for demos.

Problem:
- Patients call or email the same questions (office hours, insurance, how to book, parking).
- Front-desk staff repeat answers; after hours there is no self-service channel.

MVP:
- Chatbot that answers common clinic questions from a curated FAQ (appointments, insurance, hours, general wellness).
- Not a document RAG app — answers should come from structured FAQ content stored in the database, not uploaded PDFs.
- Streamlit UI for chat and for browsing FAQs; backend API for the UI to call.
- Light chat history so a demo conversation can continue across turns.
- Staff can maintain FAQ content (add or update entries) for demo purposes.

Must have:
- Bedrock for conversational replies grounded in FAQ content.
- Medical disclaimer on every answer (informational only; not medical advice; call 911 for emergencies).
- If nothing in the FAQ matches, say so honestly and suggest calling the clinic — do not invent clinical facts.
- Persist FAQs, chat sessions, and messages in Postgres.
- Simple dev-friendly auth for MVP; production SSO can wait.

Constraints:
- Demo-quality POC for stakeholders — not a diagnostic tool and not production HIPAA scope.
- Do not collect or store sensitive patient details (SSN, full DOB, detailed symptoms); redirect those users to call the clinic.
- No pgvector, PDF upload, or OpenSearch for v1.

Out of scope:
- Appointment booking integrations, EHR/FHIR, telehealth, multi-clinic tenancy, production HIPAA hardening.

Success:
- User asks "What are your hours?" in Streamlit and gets a grounded answer from seeded FAQ content.
- pytest passes and the app runs locally against our RDS Postgres.
