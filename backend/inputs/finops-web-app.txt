We want to build an internal FinOps Web App for engineering, finance, and platform teams using AWS-native services and AI assistance.

Current pain:
- Cloud cost data is fragmented across teams and tools, so monthly reporting is slow and often inconsistent.
- Engineers do not get timely visibility into cost anomalies, idle resources, and unit economics by service.
- Budget owners struggle to track forecast vs actual spend during the month.
- Follow-up actions from cost reviews are not consistently captured or assigned.

What we want:
- A web app that gives near real-time visibility into cloud spend, trends, and cost drivers by account, environment, team, and service.
- AI-assisted insights for anomaly explanations, optimization recommendations, and weekly FinOps summaries.
- A workflow that converts approved optimization actions into Jira tickets for engineering teams.
- Role-based access for FinOps analyst, engineering manager, team lead, and finance controller.

Initial scope (MVP):
- Cost dashboard with daily spend, month-to-date spend, and budget variance.
- Top cost drivers view (services, tags, teams, environments).
- Cost anomaly detection and alert feed with severity and probable cause.
- Optimization recommendations (rightsizing, idle resources, commitment opportunities) with confidence scores.
- Jira ticket creation from approved recommendations.
- Weekly executive summary export (Markdown/PDF) for leadership.

Data and integrations:
- AWS Cost Explorer and CUR-derived datasets as primary cost sources.
- CloudWatch metrics for workload activity context where needed.
- Jira for action tracking and ownership.
- Optional Slack notifications for anomaly alerts and weekly summaries (Phase 2).

Constraints:
- Security and compliance are critical; financial data must be role-restricted and audit-logged.
- No hardcoded credentials; all secrets through AWS Secrets Manager.
- Data in transit and at rest must be encrypted (TLS + KMS).
- Keep architecture cloud-native and operationally lightweight for MVP.

Success expectation:
- Reduce monthly cost reporting effort by at least 50 percent.
- Detect and surface high-severity anomalies within 30 minutes of data availability.
- Increase optimization action completion rate with clear ownership and due dates.
- Improve forecast accuracy and budget adherence across teams.
