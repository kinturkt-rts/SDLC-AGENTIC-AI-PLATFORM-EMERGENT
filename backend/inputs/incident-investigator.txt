We’re looking to build an AI-powered application that helps engineering teams investigate production incidents using technical documents, application logs, incident reports, and troubleshooting guides.

Users should be able to upload operational content, describe a production issue, and receive a grounded investigation summary with relevant evidence and recommended next steps.

Features:

Upload logs, runbooks, architecture documents, and previous incident reports
Organise content by application, environment, or service
Ask questions about production issues using natural language
Search across uploaded operational knowledge
Generate an incident investigation summary
Highlight likely causes and affected components
Recommend relevant troubleshooting steps from available runbooks
Identify similar historical incidents
Extract important errors, timestamps, services, and dependencies
Display references to the supporting documents or log entries
Save investigation sessions and follow-up questions
Generate a final incident report containing findings, evidence, and recommended actions
Dashboard showing recent investigations, frequently affected services, and unresolved incidents

Business Rules:

AI findings must be based on the available uploaded content.
Answers must reference the supporting source or log entry.
The application must distinguish confirmed evidence from possible causes.
When there is insufficient evidence, the application should clearly state that the cause could not be confirmed.
Recommended actions must not be executed automatically.
Deleted or restricted documents must not appear in future results.

Success:

An engineer can provide incident details and supporting operational data, receive an evidence-based analysis, identify likely causes, review related historical incidents, and generate a structured incident report without manually searching through multiple documents and logs.
