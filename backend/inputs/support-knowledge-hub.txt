Support Knowledge Hub — internal how-to articles, semantic search, duplicate awareness

Client story:
Priya runs internal IT support for a 400-person company. Answers to "how do I reset VPN?",
"expense policy for client meals", and "onboarding laptop checklist" live in scattered Confluence
pages and Slack threads. New hires search keywords and get nothing useful. Contributors keep
writing the same article twice under slightly different titles. Priya wants one hub where staff
search by meaning (not exact keywords), contributors publish vetted articles, and the system
warns when a draft looks like something already published.

MVP:
- Article catalog: title, rich text body, category (IT, HR, Finance, Facilities, General),
  optional tags, author, created/updated timestamps.
- Article lifecycle: draft → published → archived. Only published articles appear in employee
  search. Archiving hides from search but keeps history.
- Semantic search: employee types a natural-language question; system returns top matches ranked
  by meaning with a short excerpt and link to full article — not keyword-only matching.
- Similar-article check: when a contributor saves or publishes a draft, show the top few existing
  published articles that read similarly (by meaning), so they can merge or differentiate before
  publishing.
- Relevance after edits: if a published article's body changes materially, search and similarity
  should reflect the new content (not stale matches).
- Long articles: search should surface the most relevant section, not only articles whose title
  happens to match a word in the query.
- Categories browse: employees can filter search by category; contributors manage category list
  (admin only).
- Search feedback: after viewing results, employee can mark a result helpful or not — stored for
  analytics (no public leaderboard required).
- Popular gaps: analytics view listing frequent searches that returned weak or no useful matches
  (signals missing documentation).
- Pinned articles: admin can pin up to 5 published articles per category for the employee home
  screen.

Personas (who needs what):
- Employee — semantic search, read published articles, submit helpful/not-helpful on results.
- Contributor — create/edit own drafts, publish own articles, see similar-article warnings.
- Knowledge admin — publish or archive any article, manage categories, pin articles, full analytics.
- Leadership (read-only) — org-wide search analytics and coverage gaps; no content edits.
- Public/unauthenticated — health check only.

Business rules worth getting right:
- Employees never see drafts or archived articles in search/browse.
- Contributors cannot publish directly without at least title + body + category; similarity warning
  is advisory (they may still publish).
- Only knowledge admin can archive another person's article or edit another's published body.
- Archiving removes from search immediately; restoring to published re-enables search.
- Similar-article suggestions compare against published articles only (not drafts).
- Search never returns the same article twice in one result set.
- Pinning enforces max 5 per category; pinning an archived article is rejected.
- Helpful/not-helpful only allowed for published articles the employee actually opened from search.
- Leadership sees aggregated analytics only — not individual employee identities in feedback.

Demo data expectations:
- At least 5 categories with 3–4 published articles each (mix of short and long bodies).
- Several draft articles and 2–3 archived for workflow demos.
- Enough semantic overlap between pairs of articles to demo similar-article warnings
  (e.g. two VPN setup guides with different wording).
- Search analytics seed: sample queries with mixed helpful ratings and a few "weak result" gaps.
- At least 2 contributors, 1 knowledge admin, 1 leadership user, several employees.

UI expectation:
- Streamlit (or equivalent) role-gated experience:
  - Employee: home with pins + search bar + results with excerpts and feedback buttons.
  - Contributor: my drafts, editor with similar-articles panel, publish flow.
  - Knowledge admin: all articles table, category manager, pin manager, analytics dashboard.
  - Leadership: read-only analytics dashboard.
- API is source of truth; UI calls backend over HTTP.

Out of scope:
- External public internet, SSO beyond dev login, multi-language
- File attachments, video hosting, Confluence/Slack sync
- Auto-merge or block publish on similarity (warning only)
- Ticketing integration, chatbot small-talk outside article corpus
- Fine-grained per-article ACL beyond roles above
