Build a small internal Hotel Room Operations App using FastAPI and Streamlit.

Problem:

Room status and availability are currently tracked manually.
Hotel staff need one place to manage rooms and update whether they are available, occupied, or under maintenance.
Staff should use a simple Streamlit interface backed by a FastAPI service.

Who uses it:

Front desk staff use Streamlit to browse rooms and update room status.
Hotel administrators can create, update, or deactivate rooms.
Streamlit must call FastAPI and must not access the database directly.
Health checks stay open for ops.

MVP features:

Maintain rooms: room number, type, floor, capacity, nightly rate, status, active/inactive.
View one room or list all rooms.
Update room details and current status.
Filter rooms by type, floor, availability, or status.
Show available and unavailable rooms in Streamlit tables.
Seed a few sample rooms for demos.

Business rules:

Room number must be unique.
Capacity must be greater than zero.
Nightly rate cannot be negative.
Inactive rooms cannot be marked available.
Deactivated rooms remain in history.
Only administrators can create or deactivate rooms.

Must have for v1:

Persisted data.
FastAPI backend and Streamlit frontend.
Streamlit uses FastAPI only.
Basic role distinction between front desk and administrator.
Open health endpoint.

Nice to have later:

Reservations, housekeeping status, guest profiles, payments.

Constraints:

Internal use only.
Keep secrets out of source code.
Keep the UI simple and suitable for demos.

Success:

Staff can add a room, update its status, filter available rooms, deactivate it, and still retrieve the room record later.
