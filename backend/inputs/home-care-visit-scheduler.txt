Home Care Visit Scheduler — clients, caregivers, weekly visits, and missed-visit follow-up

Client story:
Maya runs a small home-care agency (about 25 caregivers). Scheduling is spreadsheets and
group chats. Families call when someone doesn’t show up, and Maya can’t see at a glance who
is overbooked or which clients still need a visit this week. She wants a simple office tool —
not a hospital system.

What the product should do:
- Keep a list of clients (name, address, preferred visit days, notes about care needs).
- Keep a caregiver roster (name, skills like mobility help or medication reminders, and whether
  they are active this week).
- Plan visits: who goes where, on which day and time window (morning / afternoon / evening),
  and how long the visit should take.
- Caregivers mark visits as completed and leave a short note about what happened.
- Office staff can cancel or reschedule visits, and reassign a different caregiver when needed.
- Flag problems: double-booked caregivers, clients with no visit scheduled this week, and
  visits that were planned but never marked complete by end of day.
- A light “today board” so the coordinator sees everyone’s load and open gaps.

Who uses it:
- Care coordinator — clients, schedule, assign/reassign, cancel, follow up on misses.
- Caregiver — see my visits for today/this week; mark complete with a short note.
- Agency owner — read-only view of the week and problem flags (no editing the schedule).

Rules that matter in real life:
- A caregiver should not be scheduled for two overlapping visits.
- Only active caregivers can be assigned.
- Completed visits should not be casually rewritten; coordinators may add a follow-up note.
- Cancelled visits stay in history so the family conversation is auditable.
- Prefer showing “who still needs coverage this week” without burying people in reports.

Demo data expectations:
- Several clients and caregivers with different skills.
- A week’s worth of visits across statuses (planned, completed, cancelled, missed).
- At least one double-book risk or missed visit to show on the board.

Out of scope:
- GPS tracking, payroll, billing families, clinical charting, mobile app for caregivers
