Hi Team,

We need an internal application to manage employee training, mandatory courses, and professional certifications. Currently, HR and department managers track completion dates in separate spreadsheets, so it is difficult to know who has completed required training and whose certification is about to expire.

The application should use Streamlit for the interface and FastAPI for the backend. HR should manage courses and certification requirements, managers should monitor their teams, and employees should view their own assigned training.

Features
Maintain training courses and certification types
Assign courses to employees or departments
Track enrolment, completion status, score, and completion date
Upload or record certificate details
Track certification issue and expiry dates
Dashboard showing overdue training, upcoming expiries, completion rates, and department progress
Search and filter by employee, department, course, certification, status, and due date
Managers can view training progress for their own teams
Employees can view assigned and completed training
Record training history
Export completion and compliance reports to CSV
Role-based views for HR, manager, and employee
Audit history for assignment and completion changes
Important business rules
Completion dates cannot be earlier than assignment dates.
Expiry dates must be later than issue dates.
Completed training records must remain in history.
Employees should not receive duplicate active assignments for the same course.
Only HR users can create courses or modify certification requirements.
Managers can view only employees in their departments.
Expired certifications must be clearly marked as non-compliant.
Technical expectations
FastAPI backend with CRUD APIs
Streamlit frontend using FastAPI only
Persisted relational database
Related entities such as employees, departments, courses, assignments, certifications, and completion records
Validation and clear error handling
Seed data for demonstration
Open health-check endpoint
No hard-coded secrets
Success

HR can create a mandatory course, assign it to a department, record employee completions, identify overdue users, monitor expiring certifications, and export a compliance report.
