We’re looking to build a backend application that helps a regional banking network manage customers, accounts, beneficiaries, transfers, service requests, branch operations, and transaction reviews from one central platform.
The application should be built with FastAPI and should expose a well-structured API that can later support web, mobile, and internal operations tools.
 
Scenario
The bank currently uses separate systems for customer onboarding, account servicing, beneficiary management, fund transfers, and branch requests. This creates duplicate records, inconsistent validation, and limited visibility into the full customer journey.
The new system should provide a unified banking operations layer while preserving complete history for financial and audit purposes.
 
Features
Maintain customer profiles with personal details, contact information, identity references, risk level, and customer status
Support individual and business customers
Maintain multiple addresses and contact methods for each customer
Create and manage bank branches
Open and manage savings, checking, and business accounts
Track account status, available balance, ledger balance, currency, and branch ownership
Support joint account holders and account ownership percentages
Freeze, unfreeze, close, or reactivate accounts through controlled workflows
Maintain beneficiaries for domestic transfers
Verify and activate new beneficiaries before they can be used
Initiate transfers between a customer’s own accounts
Initiate transfers to registered beneficiaries
Support scheduled and recurring transfers
Track transfer states such as Draft, Pending Review, Processing, Completed, Rejected, Cancelled, and Failed
Record transaction history, reference numbers, amounts, fees, timestamps, and descriptions
Support deposits, withdrawals, transfers, fees, reversals, and adjustments
Allow authorized operations users to place transactions under review
Record review notes, approval decisions, and rejection reasons
Handle transaction reversals without deleting the original transaction
Maintain daily transfer limits by customer type and risk level
Maintain account-level restrictions and blocked transaction types
Create service requests for address changes, account statements, cheque books, card replacement, and account closure
Assign service requests to branches or operations teams
Track request status, priority, owner, comments, and resolution details
Search and filter customers, accounts, transactions, transfers, beneficiaries, and service requests
Provide account statements for selected date ranges
Provide summary information for customer balances, recent transactions, pending transfers, and open service requests
Provide operational summaries for branch activity, failed transactions, pending reviews, and high-value transfers
Maintain complete audit history for important actions and state changes
Seed realistic sample customers, accounts, beneficiaries, transfers, transactions, branches, and service requests
Important Business Rules
Customer identifiers and verified email addresses must be unique where applicable.
Closed customers cannot open new accounts.
Closed or frozen accounts cannot initiate outgoing transfers.
Account balances must never become negative unless the account explicitly supports an overdraft facility.
Available balance and ledger balance must be tracked separately.
Joint account ownership percentages must total 100 percent.
A beneficiary must be verified and active before receiving a transfer.
A customer cannot register the same beneficiary account more than once.
Transfer amounts must be positive and within the customer’s remaining daily limit.
High-value transfers may require additional review before processing.
Scheduled transfers cannot use dates in the past.
Recurring transfers must include a valid frequency and end condition.
A completed financial transaction cannot be edited or deleted.
Corrections must be handled through reversals or adjustment entries.
Reversing a transaction must create linked financial records and preserve the original entry.
Duplicate transfer submissions with the same idempotency reference must not create duplicate transactions.
Account closure must be rejected when the account has a non-zero balance, pending transfer, active restriction, or unresolved service request.
Rejected and cancelled transfers must remain available in history.
Service requests must follow valid status transitions.
Closed service requests cannot be modified unless reopened by an authorized user.
Sensitive customer and transaction data must only be available to authorized roles.
Every change to customer status, account status, limits, restrictions, transfers, and financial records must be auditable.
