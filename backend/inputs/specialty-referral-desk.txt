Specialty Referral Desk — patients, referring clinics, specialist handoffs, and follow-up

Client story:
A mid-size family clinic sends patients to outside specialists every week. Today it is fax
coversheets and sticky notes. The office manager wants a desk tool to log each referral,
track whether the specialist visit happened, and chase open loops — not a full EHR.

MVP:
- Patients: name, date of birth, preferred phone, optional MRN-style chart number.
- Referring providers (doctors/NPs at this clinic) and destination specialties
  (cardiology, ortho, dermatology, etc.).
- Referral records: patient, referring provider, specialty, reason for referral (short text),
  urgency (routine / soon / urgent), created date, and lifecycle status from drafted through
  scheduled, completed, cancelled, or lost-to-follow-up.
- Optional specialist practice name and appointment date once known.
- Staff can add follow-up notes and mark when the visit was confirmed completed.
- Queue views: open referrals by urgency; referrals waiting more than N days without an
  appointment date; completed this month.
- Audit trail of status changes and note additions.

Personas (who needs what):
- Referral coordinator — create referrals, update status, add appointment details, notes.
- Clinician (referring provider) — see their own open referrals; add clinical reason notes;
  cannot cancel another provider’s referral.
- Clinic manager — all referrals, queues, and monthly completion counts; can reassign
  coordinator ownership if needed.
- Public/unauthenticated — health check only.

Business rules worth getting right:
- Cannot mark completed without either an appointment date or an explicit “completed without
  scheduled date” note (rare path — require a reason).
- Urgent referrals should surface on a priority board if still open after 3 business days
  without an appointment date.
- Cancelling requires a short reason; cancelled and completed are terminal.
- Lost-to-follow-up only allowed from open states after staff confirms outreach attempts
  (capture a brief outreach note).
- Same patient can have multiple referrals; do not block duplicates, but show prior open
  referrals for that patient on the detail screen as a warning.

Demo data expectations:
- Several providers, specialties, ~20 patients, ~30 referrals across statuses.
- Mix of urgent and routine; at least two “stuck” open referrals for the chase queue.
- A few completed referrals with appointment dates and notes.

UI expectation:
- Simple web UI: open queue, patient/referral detail, manager summary counts.
  API is the source of truth.

Out of scope:
- Insurance authorizations, clinical document upload, e-prescribing, patient portal login,
  HIPAA compliance program beyond basic access roles
