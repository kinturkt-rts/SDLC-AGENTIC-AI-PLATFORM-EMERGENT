Office Equipment Checkout — track laptops, monitors, and accessories lent to staff

Problem:
IT keeps a closet of spare laptops, monitors, keyboards, and adapters for new hires and
travel. Today they use a shared spreadsheet that is always out of date — nobody knows
what is available, who has what, or when something is overdue.

Who uses it:
- IT admins maintain the equipment catalog and record checkouts and returns.
- Employees can view what they currently have checked out (read-only for v1).
- Only signed-in users access equipment records; health checks for ops can stay open.

MVP features:
- Sign in with username and password. IT admins and employees get different permissions.
- Equipment catalog: each item has a label, category (laptop, monitor, keyboard, adapter, other),
  serial number or asset tag, and status (available, checked out, maintenance, retired).
- Checkout flow: admin assigns an available item to an employee with a due date and optional note.
- Return flow: admin marks an item returned; status goes back to available.
- List all items with filters by status, category, and who has it checked out.
- Highlight overdue checkouts (due date in the past and not yet returned).
- Simple checkout history per item (who had it, checkout date, return date).

Business rules:
- An item in checked-out or maintenance status cannot be checked out again until returned
  or marked available.
- Retired items cannot be checked out.
- Due date must be today or in the future at checkout time.
- Employees cannot check out items themselves in v1 — admin only.
- Inactive users should not be able to sign in.

Must have for v1:
- Persisted data with seed users for demo (at least one IT admin and two employees).
- Seed a small catalog (roughly eight items across categories) and one active checkout.
- Role-based access so employees cannot modify the catalog or checkouts.
- API suitable for demos (Swagger is fine); no customer-facing web UI required yet.

Nice to have (later):
- Self-service checkout requests, barcode labels, email overdue reminders, asset photos.

Constraints:
- Internal team use only.
- Keep secrets out of source code.

Success:
- Admin checks out a laptop to an employee, employee sees it on their list, admin returns it,
  item shows available again, and overdue items appear on an admin report.
