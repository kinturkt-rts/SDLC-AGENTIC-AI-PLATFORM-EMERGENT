Build a medium internal Training Session Registration App using FastAPI and Streamlit.

Problem:

Employees register for internal training through email, and organizers manually track seats and cancellations.
Teams need one place to publish sessions, register employees, prevent overbooking, and see attendance lists.
Employees should use a simple Streamlit interface backed by a clean FastAPI service.

Who uses it:

Employees can browse upcoming sessions, register, cancel, and view their registrations.
Training coordinators can create sessions, update capacity, close registration, and view attendees.
Streamlit must call FastAPI and must not connect directly to the database.
Health checks stay open for ops.

MVP features:

Maintain sessions: title, trainer, date, start time, location, capacity, status.
Register an employee with name and email.
Cancel a registration using soft cancellation.
List and filter sessions by date, trainer, status, or location.
Show available seats and attendee lists.
Seed a few upcoming sessions and sample registrations.

Business rules:

An employee cannot have more than one active registration for the same session.
Registration is rejected when capacity is reached.
Employees cannot register for cancelled or closed sessions.
Session date and time must be in the future when created.
Capacity cannot be reduced below the number of active registrations.
Cancelled registrations remain in history.
Only coordinators can create, update, or cancel sessions.

Must have for v1:

Persisted data.
FastAPI backend and Streamlit frontend.
Role distinction between employee and coordinator.
Streamlit uses FastAPI only.
Open health endpoint.

Nice to have later:

Waitlists, calendar invites, attendance check-in, completion certificates.

Constraints:

Internal use only.
Keep secrets out of source code.
Keep the UI simple and demo-friendly.

Success:

An employee can register for an available session, duplicate registration is rejected, registration stops when capacity is full, cancellation frees a seat, and coordinators can still view cancelled history.
