Vendor Compliance Tracker for procurement and legal.

Client story:
Maria in Procurement missed a contract renewal; Legal needs proof that current insurance and SOC documents are on file. About forty vendors are tracked today mostly by email. v1 is internal only — no vendor-facing portal.

Problem:
- Contract end dates and compliance paperwork are easy to miss.
- Nobody has one place to see what is expiring, expired, or missing for a vendor.
- Viewers should see status without downloading sensitive files; admins manage everything.

Who uses it:
- Admins (procurement/legal) register vendors and contracts, upload compliance documents, drive renewal status, and download files.
- Viewers can browse vendors, contracts, document metadata, and alerts — but cannot upload, delete, or download raw files when marked sensitive.
- Health checks stay open for ops. Sign-in with username and password (admin vs viewer roles).

MVP features:
- Register vendors (legal name, category, contact, risk tier) and their contracts (dates, value, auto-renew, owner, notes).
- Attach compliance documents with type and expiry (e.g. insurance certificate, SOC 2, MSA, W-9); keep version history when a new file replaces an old one.
- Renewal workflow per contract: not started → in review → renewed, terminated, or lapsed — with a short history of who changed status and why.
- Alerts: contracts and documents expiring in 30 / 60 / 90 days; expired items; vendors missing required document types.
- Simple UI tabs for vendor registry, contract timeline, compliance gaps, and expiring alerts.
- Seed enough sample vendors, contracts, and documents (including a few near expiry) for a credible demo.

Business rules:
- A contract cannot be marked renewed unless its end date has been extended.
- Uploading a new document of the same type for a vendor supersedes the previous current version.
- Terminated contracts stay locked unless an admin reopens them into review.
- Viewers must not download raw files; admins can.
- Software and services vendors should have insurance and SOC-style docs on file when those types are marked required.

Must have for v1:
- Persisted data and role-based access (admin vs viewer).
- File uploads stored safely for the demo (local storage is fine).
- A usable internal UI — not spreadsheet-only.

Nice to have (later):
- DocuSign, ERP sync, email reminders, AI risk scoring, vendor self-service portal.

Constraints:
- Internal use only.
- Keep secrets out of source code.

Success:
- Maria sees contracts and docs expiring soon, opens a vendor’s compliance gaps, uploads a renewed certificate, and moves a contract through renewal with an audit note.
- A viewer can see the same alerts and metadata but cannot download or change files.
