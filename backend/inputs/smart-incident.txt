We’re looking to build a backend application that helps organisations report, assign, track, and resolve operational incidents. The system should provide a structured workflow for managing incidents from initial reporting through resolution.

Features:
Create and manage incidents
Assign incidents to teams or engineers
Set severity, category, and priority
Track statuses such as Open, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, and Closed
Add investigation notes and resolution details
Maintain a complete incident activity history
Search and filter incidents by status, severity, owner, category, or date
Escalate incidents that remain unresolved beyond their target time
Provide summary APIs for active incidents, critical incidents, and average resolution time

Business Rules:
Critical incidents must be assigned the highest priority.
Resolved incidents must include a resolution summary.
Closed incidents cannot be edited unless reopened.
Every assignment and status change must be retained in the activity history.
An incident cannot be closed before it is resolved.
Success

Teams can report incidents, assign ownership, track investigation progress, record resolutions, and retrieve operational metrics through REST APIs.
