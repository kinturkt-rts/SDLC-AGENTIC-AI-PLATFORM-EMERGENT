Lunch & Learn Sessions for our company.

Problem:
- Internal Lunch & Learn talks are scheduled in spreadsheets and chat threads.
- Organizers lose track of seat limits and overbook rooms.
- Employees want an easy way to see upcoming sessions and sign up or cancel.

Who uses it:
- Organizers create and update sessions (title, host, time, seat limit) and see who registered — protected by a shared API key.
- Employees browse open sessions and register or cancel their own signup with name and email — no personal login for v1.
- Anyone can list sessions and check health; writes need the organizer API key.

MVP features:
- Create sessions with a maximum number of seats and a scheduled time.
- Employees register for a session; block duplicate signups for the same email on the same session.
- When seats fill up, mark the session full; when someone cancels, free a seat again.
- Organizers can cancel a whole session; nobody should register for a cancelled session.
- Soft-cancel a registration instead of wiping history.
- Seed a couple of sample sessions (including one nearly full and one cancelled) for demos.
- Backend/API only for v1 — Swagger is fine; no browser UI yet.

Business rules:
- Cannot reduce seat limit below how many people are already confirmed.
- Session time should be in the future when created.
- Email uniqueness for a session should ignore case.
- Show how many seats remain on each session.

Must have for v1:
- Persisted data (not in-memory only).
- Clear capacity and overbooking protection.

Nice to have (later):
- Browser UI, waitlists, email reminders, personal logins.

Constraints:
- Internal use only.
- Keep secrets out of source code.

Success:
- Organizer creates a small session; employees fill it until full; one cancels and a seat opens; cancelled sessions reject new signups.
