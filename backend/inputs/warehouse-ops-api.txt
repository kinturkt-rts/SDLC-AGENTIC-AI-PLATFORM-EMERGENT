We're looking to build a backend application that helps warehouse teams manage inventory, inbound shipments, outbound orders, and stock movements across multiple warehouse locations. The goal is to improve inventory visibility and reduce manual tracking.

Features

Manage warehouses, storage locations, and inventory
Register products with categories and suppliers
Record inbound shipments and stock receipts
Process customer orders and outbound shipments
Transfer inventory between warehouse locations
Track stock movements and inventory history
Maintain reorder thresholds for products
Search inventory by product, supplier, location, or status
View inventory availability and reserved stock
Dashboard APIs for inventory levels, low-stock items, inbound shipments, and order fulfillment

Business Rules

Stock cannot become negative.
Orders cannot be fulfilled if sufficient inventory is unavailable.
Inventory transfers must update both source and destination locations.
Every inventory movement should be recorded for audit purposes.
Cancelled shipments should restore reserved inventory.

Success

Warehouse teams can efficiently manage inventory, process shipments, monitor stock availability, and maintain complete inventory movement history through REST APIs.
