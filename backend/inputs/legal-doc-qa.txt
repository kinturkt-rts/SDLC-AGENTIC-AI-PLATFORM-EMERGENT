Legal Contract Q&A — ask questions across uploaded vendor contracts

Client story:
Priya runs legal operations for a 400-person company. Her team maintains several hundred
executed agreements in a shared drive — vendor MSAs, NDAs, SOWs, renewal letters, and
amendments. Every quarter, finance asks which contracts auto-renew in the next 90 days,
sales wants to know assignment restrictions before a deal closes, and leadership wants
quick answers on termination notice periods without waiting for a full contract review.

Today Priya or a paralegal opens 40–60 page PDFs and searches manually. Answers take
hours and are easy to get wrong under time pressure. She wants an internal tool where
the team uploads contracts once, then anyone with access can ask questions in plain
English and get answers tied back to the exact clause in the source document.

What the tool should do (MVP):
- Legal ops uploads PDF contracts and tags each one with a type (NDA, MSA, SOW, or other)
  and a readable title (e.g. "Acme Corp MSA 2024").
- Approved readers browse the document library — see filename, type, page count, upload date.
- Readers ask questions in a chat-style screen. Examples:
  - "What is the termination notice period for the Acme MSA?"
  - "Which NDAs expire in Q3 2026?"
  - "Can we assign the Globex SOW to a subsidiary?"
- Every answer must cite where it came from: document title, page number, and a short
  quote from the PDF text. If the system cannot find supporting text, it should refuse
  clearly — no guessing, no answers from general legal knowledge outside our files.
- Legal ops can delete a document when it is superseded; related indexed content should
  go away with it.
- Keep a simple history of recent questions and answers so users can revisit what was asked.

Who uses it:
- Legal ops (Priya's team) — upload, tag, delete documents; full library access.
- Business reader (finance, sales, ops) — ask questions and browse the library; cannot
  upload or delete.
- We are fine with shared access keys for MVP — no full SSO or user directory yet.

Behavior we care about:
- Answers come only from uploaded PDF text. If confidence is low, say "I cannot answer
  from the documents on file" and suggest rephrasing or uploading missing agreements.
- Citations must be specific enough that Priya can verify in under a minute.
- Only digital PDFs with selectable text for v1 — scanned image PDFs are out of scope.
- Reasonable file size limits so someone does not accidentally upload a 200 MB archive.

Demo expectations:
- Seed or upload at least three sample contracts (one NDA, one MSA, one SOW) so a demo
  question about termination or renewal returns a cited answer.
- Show one question that correctly refuses because the topic is not in the library.
- A simple web UI with upload for legal ops, a Q&A chat area, a document list, and
  recent question history — organizers should not need Swagger for day-to-day use.

Out of scope for v1:
- Side-by-side comparison of two contract versions
- Redlining, editing clauses, or e-signature workflows
- OCR for scanned documents
- Multi-tenant or external customer access
- Automated legal advice disclaimers beyond a simple "for internal reference only" note

Success looks like:
- Priya uploads a vendor MSA, asks about termination, and gets an answer with page and
  snippet she can click through to verify.
- A sales user asks about a vendor not in the library and gets an honest refusal, not
  a fabricated clause.
- The team uses it in a stakeholder demo without opening PDFs manually.
