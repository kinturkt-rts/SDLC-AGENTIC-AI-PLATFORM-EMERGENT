We need an internal AI system that records meeting transcripts, generates summaries, extracts action items, and tracks follow-ups.

Current pain:
- Meeting notes are scattered across Teams recordings, shared documents, and personal notebooks.
- Action items often get forgotten because there is no central tracking.
- Managers spend time manually summarizing meetings and following up with team members.

What we want:
- Upload meeting transcripts or recordings (transcripts can be generated separately for MVP).
- Automatically generate concise meeting summaries.
- Extract action items, owners, due dates, and decisions.
- Search past meetings using natural-language questions.
- Dashboard showing pending, completed, and overdue action items.

Initial scope:
Upload meeting transcript files (PDF, DOCX, or TXT).
AI-generated summary of each meeting.
Extract:
    Action items
    Owners
    Due dates
    Key decisions
Search meetings by keyword or natural-language query.
View meeting history and action-item status.
Audit log of uploads and generated summaries.

Data and integrations:
- Object storage for uploaded transcripts.
- Relational database for meetings and action items.
- LLM for summarization and extraction (prefer Amazon Bedrock).

Constraints:
- Meeting data is confidential.
- Access should be restricted to authorized users.
- Generated summaries should reference the original meeting transcript when possible.
- Secrets managed securely.
- MVP is internal use only.

Success expectation:
- Users can upload a transcript and receive a summary with action items in under a minute.
- Managers can quickly review previous meetings.
- Teams reduce missed follow-ups and manual note-taking.`
