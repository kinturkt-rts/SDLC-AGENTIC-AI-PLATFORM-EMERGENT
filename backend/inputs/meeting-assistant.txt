Build an AI meeting assistant for our internal team.

Problem:
- People waste time finding free slots across calendars.
- Reminders are manual and easy to miss.
- Follow-ups after meetings are inconsistent.

MVP feature:
- User asks in plain English: "Schedule a 30-min sync with Alex and Priya next week about project kickoff."
- Agent checks calendar availability, proposes 2–3 time options, and creates the event after user picks one.
- Agent sends meeting invites and sets reminders (15 min before + day before).
- User can ask: "Remind me to send notes after tomorrow's standup."

Must have:
- Connect to Microsoft 365 / Outlook calendar (read + create events).
- Chat or simple web form for requests.
- Store meeting intent, chosen slot, and reminder jobs.
- Audit log of what the agent scheduled or changed.

Nice to have (later):
- Slack/Teams notifications.
- Auto-reschedule when conflicts appear.

Constraints:
- Internal users only.
- No access to calendars outside approved org accounts.
- Ask for confirmation before creating or deleting events.
- Secrets in managed store only.

Success:
- Most scheduling requests completed in under 2 minutes.
- Reminders delivered reliably.
- Users can see a history of agent actions.
