We’re looking to build a patient-friendly web application that helps users understand healthcare information, prepare for appointments, and find the right next step using trusted medical and clinic resources.

The application should provide a Streamlit interface with a backend API for document management, search, and AI responses. Healthcare organizations should be able to upload approved FAQs, care instructions, service guides, preparation documents, and patient education materials.

Scenario:
Patients often receive information from multiple sources and may not know where to look when they have questions such as:

    How should I prepare for an upcoming procedure?
    What documents should I bring to my appointment?
    Which department should I contact?
    What should I expect after a routine treatment?
    Where can I find billing, insurance, or visiting information?

The assistant should help users find clear answers without requiring them to manually search through long documents or multiple web pages.

Features:

Ask healthcare and clinic-related questions in natural language
Search approved patient FAQs and education materials
Provide answers with links or references to the supporting source
Browse common topics such as appointments, procedures, medications, billing, insurance, and recovery guidance
Generate simple summaries of lengthy patient instructions
Create a personalized appointment preparation checklist
Help users identify the appropriate clinic, department, or support service
Allow users to save useful answers and checklists
Provide multilingual-friendly responses where supported
Collect optional feedback on whether an answer was helpful
Show popular questions and recently updated guidance
Admin area for uploading, updating, categorizing, and retiring content
Dashboard showing common patient questions, unanswered topics, and feedback trends

Business Rules:

Responses must be grounded in approved healthcare content.
Every answer should reference the supporting document or FAQ.
The assistant must not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice.
Urgent or emergency-related questions should direct users to appropriate emergency services.
When an answer cannot be confirmed, the assistant should clearly say so and recommend contacting the relevant healthcare provider.
Retired or deleted content must not appear in future responses.
Users should not be required to provide sensitive medical information to use the general FAQ experience.

Success:

A patient can open the application, ask a question in everyday language, receive a clear and trustworthy answer with supporting references, generate an appointment checklist, and quickly find the right department or next step.
