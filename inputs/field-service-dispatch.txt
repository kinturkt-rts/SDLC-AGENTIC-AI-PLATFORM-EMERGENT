Field Service Dispatch — customers, work orders, technician assignment, SLA board

Client story:
Dana runs a 15-tech HVAC shop; dispatch is whiteboard + group text. She needs an office tool for
assignments, status updates, and "who is overloaded today" — not Salesforce.

MVP:
- Customer records with contact info and service address.
- Work orders linked to customers: description, priority (routine vs urgent), scheduled date,
  time window (morning / afternoon / all day), and lifecycle status from new through completed
  or cancelled.
- Technician roster with skills (e.g. residential, commercial, install) and active flag.
- Dispatcher assigns one active technician per order; can reassign; sees today's board.
- Technicians update status only on jobs assigned to them.
- Owner role: read-only visibility into board and workload — no dispatch writes.
- SLA idea: urgent jobs should complete same day; surface breached SLAs on today's board.
- On completion: capture brief completion notes and line items for parts used (name, qty, optional cost).
- History of status changes (who changed what, when) for disputes.

Personas (who needs what):
- Dispatcher — customers, orders, assignments, reschedule, cancel.
- Technician — my jobs today, progress updates, add parts on active jobs.
- Owner — dashboards and board; read-only.
- Public/unauthenticated — health check only.

Business rules worth getting right:
- Cannot assign unless order is in a dispatchable state; only one active assignment per order.
- Technician cannot touch another tech's active assignment.
- Completing a job requires meaningful completion notes; parts list can be empty but allowed.
- Cancelling allowed from non-terminal states; completed jobs are effectively locked except
  dispatcher addendum on notes.
- Urgent priority implies a same-day completion expectation — system should flag when that
  window is missed relative to scheduled date.
- Board for a given day: unassigned queue, per-technician columns, SLA breaches highlighted.

Demo data expectations:
- Several technicians with different skills, multiple customers, ~15 orders across statuses.
- Mix of urgent and routine; at least one breached SLA for dashboard demo.
- Assignments and parts on a few completed jobs.

UI expectation:
- Streamlit (or equivalent): dispatcher board, technician "my jobs" with status actions.
  API is source of truth.

Out of scope:
- GPS tracking, SMS to customers, payments, inventory stock depletion
