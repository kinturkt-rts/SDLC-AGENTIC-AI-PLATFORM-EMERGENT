We want to build an internal AI Operations Assistant for cloud teams using Amazon Bedrock and agent-based workflows.

Current pain:
- Teams manually investigate CloudWatch alarms and spend too much time collecting logs and context.
- Incident summaries are inconsistent and handoffs across teams are slow.
- There is no standard way to capture actions and follow-up tasks after incidents.

What we want:
- An assistant that can answer operational questions, summarize incidents, and suggest next actions.
- It should use Bedrock models and support agent-style orchestration (planner + specialist agents).
- It should connect with AWS services for operational context: CloudWatch logs/metrics and optionally Jira for task tracking.
- It should support role-based usage (SRE, on-call engineer, engineering manager).

Initial scope:
- Incident summary generation from provided incident context.
- Recommended remediation checklist generation.
- Ability to convert approved actions into Jira tickets.
- Simple chat/API interface for internal users.

Constraints:
- Security and compliance are critical because logs may contain sensitive operational data.
- We prefer AWS-native services and minimal operational overhead.
- The solution should be production-ready in phases (MVP first, then scaling).

Success expectation:
- Faster incident triage and clearer action tracking.
- Better consistency in incident reporting and follow-up ownership.
