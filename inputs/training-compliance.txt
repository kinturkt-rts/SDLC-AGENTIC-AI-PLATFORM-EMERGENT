Training & Certification Compliance — mandatory courses, expirations, team gaps

Client story:
Marcus (HR) is audited every quarter on who completed safety and security training. Today it is
spreadsheets and inbox searches. Managers cannot see which direct reports are out of compliance
until someone escalates. Marcus needs one place to define required trainings, record completions,
and surface who is missing or about to expire — without managers editing other teams' records.

MVP:
- Course catalog: name, category (safety, security, role-specific), validity period in months
  (e.g. annual refresh), and whether it is required for all staff or for selected job roles.
- Employee roster with department, manager, job role, and active/inactive flag.
- Record training completions with completion date; system calculates expiry from validity period.
- Recertification: a new completion replaces the prior active record for that person + course.
- Required-course matrix: each job role lists which courses are mandatory.
- Compliance view per employee: required vs completed vs expired vs never taken.
- Manager view: own team's compliance summary only — not other departments.
- HR admin: full catalog, roster, record completions for anyone, deactivate employees.
- Compliance officer (read-only): org-wide dashboards — overdue, expiring within 30 days,
  completion rate by department, worst gaps by course.
- Alerts conceptually: employee with expired required course; employee inactive but still showing
  as compliant owner on open items (should drop from active compliance counts).

Personas (who needs what):
- HR administrator — defines courses and role requirements, manages roster, records completions.
- Manager — sees team compliance; cannot change catalog or other teams.
- Employee — sees own required and completed trainings; cannot mark others complete.
- Compliance officer — read-only org reports and alerts; no writes.
- Public/unauthenticated — health check only for ops.

Business rules worth getting right:
- Inactive employees excluded from compliance percentages and manager team totals.
- A course required for a role applies to everyone currently in that role; changing role changes
  the requirement set going forward (no retroactive penalty for courses no longer required).
- Expired = completion exists but expiry date is before today.
- Missing = required course has no current non-expired completion.
- Managers only see employees where they are the recorded manager.
- Deactivating an employee does not delete history; they disappear from active compliance views.
- Optional: soft cap — flag if someone has more than 20 active course records (data quality guard).

Demo data expectations:
- Enough courses to cover all-vs-role-specific rules (at least 6 courses, 3 role types).
- At least 15 employees across 3 departments with mixed compliance states.
- Several expiring-within-30-days and several never-started required courses for dashboard demos.
- At least one inactive employee still referenced in history.

UI expectation:
- Streamlit (or equivalent) role-gated screens: HR catalog & roster, manager team board,
  employee "my trainings", compliance officer dashboard. API remains the source of truth.

Out of scope:
- LMS integration, SCORM, video hosting, payments
- Email/SMS reminders (show in-app alerts only)
- External certificate file storage beyond a simple reference field
- Multi-language, multi-tenant
